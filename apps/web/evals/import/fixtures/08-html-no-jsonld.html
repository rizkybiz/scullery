Grandma's Oat Cookies

Makes about 24 cookies.

Ingredients

  • 115 g butter, softened
  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 150 g rolled oats
  • 120 g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar, then beat in the egg.
  3. Stir in the oats, flour and baking soda.
  4. Roll into balls and bake for 12 minutes.
