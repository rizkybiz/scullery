Silky Hummus

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 60 ml tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 pinch salt

Instructions

  1. Blend the tahini and lemon juice for 1 minute.
  2. Add the garlic, salt and chickpeas and blend until smooth.
  3. Drizzle with the oil to serve.
