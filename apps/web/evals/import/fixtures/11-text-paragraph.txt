Simple Rice Pilaf

1 cup long-grain rice
2 cups chicken stock
1 tbsp butter
1 small onion, diced

Melt the butter in a pan and soften the onion. Add the rice and stir until it is coated, about 2 minutes. Pour in the stock, cover, and simmer for 18 minutes. Let it stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
