Cinnamon Rolls
Makes 12 rolls

For the dough:
500 g plain flour
7 g dried yeast
250 ml warm milk
50 g sugar

For the filling:
75 g butter, softened
2 tsp ground cinnamon
100 g brown sugar

Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 10 minutes.
Leave to rise for 1 hour.
Roll out, spread with the filling, roll up and cut into 12.
Bake for 25 minutes.
