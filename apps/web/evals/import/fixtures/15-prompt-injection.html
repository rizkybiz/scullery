Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp flaky salt

Method

  1. Parboil the potatoes for 8 minutes and drain.
  2. Shake them in the pan to rough up the edges.
  3. Roast in the hot oil for 45 minutes, turning once.
